# Privacy Policy

Tiny SFTP Server is engineered from the ground up with a strict privacy-first principle:

- No Data Collection: The app does not collect, track, aggregate, or transmit any user information or telemetry.
- Zero Remote Storage: There are no external databases or analytics servers. Everything runs entirely locally on your Mac.
- Local Direct Network Transfers: All SFTP file transfers happen directly between your SFTP client and your Mac on your local network. No middleman servers are ever involved.
- Security-Scoped Sandboxing: The app only accesses the specific shared folder you explicitly choose using macOS security-scoped permissions.
